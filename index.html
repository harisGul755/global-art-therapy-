Questions

Good to know

Do I need to be good at art?

Not at all. Not everyone is comfortable with art, so talk therapy methods are used as well, with art materials always close by.

Who do you work with?

Children, adolescents, families, adults, trauma survivors and active-duty military.

Where do you see clients?

Face to face in Asheville, NC, and online for clients in NC, SC, GA and FL.
