Biography

Dr. Alexis Decosimo, DrPH, ATR

Alexis is a licensed mental health clinician, registered art therapist and Doctor of Public Health. She earned her master's in Art Therapy and Clinical Counseling at The George Washington University and her doctorate in public health at East Tennessee State University.

She specializes in trauma-informed art therapy, program development and international development.

Philosophy

My approach

I see my role as a supportive ally and advocate, focused on strengths, empowerment and coping skills. I believe a therapist should be a safe, non-judgmental place for a client, and I encourage honesty and collaboration.
